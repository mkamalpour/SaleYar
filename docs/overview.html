THE SELECTED STAGE

Upload and map

Open the owner dashboard and upload CSV or Excel invoices. Review recognized columns before analysis.

Trace the transactions

Inspect sales, purchases, returns and damaged stock. Explicit transaction types determine direction; rows without a type default to sales.

Review demand and baskets

Compare product metrics, labelled forecast support, directional basket confidence and customer groups.

Plan within the budget

Choose profit, customer coverage or risk goals. Inspect suggested quantities and assumptions before making a real-world purchase separately.

05 / THE EVIDENCE

What was checked. What remains open.

Reliable documentation makes the boundary visible rather than promising perfection.

90 synthetic offline tests passed in existing Conda base. The alternate saleYar environment encountered a native dependency crash and is not certified by this run; no environment changes made.

  • Forecasts, risk scores and purchase suggestions are estimates, not business guarantees.
  • Missing records, stockouts and closures can distort inferred demand. Basket association does not imply causation.
